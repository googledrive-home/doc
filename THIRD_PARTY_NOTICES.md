## Formula 1 circuit centre-line data

The GPS centre-line coordinates used for Spa-Francorchamps, Monza, Silverstone, Suzuka and Monaco in `formula-one.html` are adapted from the GeoJSON circuit data maintained by Tomislav Bacinger:

- Project: https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits
- Source files: `circuits/be-1925.geojson`, `circuits/it-1922.geojson`, `circuits/gb-1948.geojson`, `circuits/jp-1962.geojson`, and `circuits/mc-1929.geojson`
- Copyright: Copyright (c) 2019-2025 Tomislav Bacinger
- Licence: MIT

The source coordinates are projected from longitude/latitude into a local metre-based coordinate frame for the simulation, then sampled by the game engine. The centre-line source is not a full 3D elevation or venue asset pack.

## Laguna Seca circuit geometry

The Laguna Seca GP centre-line in `formula-one.html` is derived from the `gp` midline in `tobi/track-atlas` (`tracks/laguna-seca/raw/layers/gp.geojson`). The source project's attribution states that this geometry is derived from OpenStreetMap via Overpass.

- Source project: https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas
- Source data: https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/blob/main/tracks/laguna-seca/raw/layers/gp.geojson
- Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors
- Data licence: Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0 — https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- The source repository distinguishes MIT-licensed code from ODbL-licensed geometry. The Laguna Seca centre-line is distributed under ODbL terms and is not covered by the MIT notice above.

The source midline contains 593 mapped points and is projected into a local metre-based coordinate frame. The existing in-game elevation samples are retained to approximate the Corkscrew and surrounding elevation changes. Kerbs, run-off, barriers and venue scenery remain generated by the game.

## Spa-Francorchamps circuit geometry

The Spa GP centre-line in `formula-one.html` is derived from the `gp` outline in `tobi/track-atlas` (`tracks/spa-francorchamps/raw/layers/gp.geojson`). The source project identifies it as the Grand Prix Circuit centre-line from OpenStreetMap relation 284560.

- Source project: https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas
- Source data: https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/blob/main/tracks/spa-francorchamps/raw/layers/gp.geojson
- Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors
- Data licence: Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0 — https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The mapped centre-line is projected into a local metre-based coordinate frame and scaled to the 7,004 m game lap length. The existing in-game Spa elevation profile is retained.


## OpenStreetMap-derived racing circuit geometry (ODbL 1.0)

The circuit point arrays in `formula-one.html` for Spa-Francorchamps, Monza, Laguna Seca, Silverstone, Monaco and Suzuka are adapted from the corresponding GP layout outline GeoJSON files in the `tobi/track-atlas` project. Its README documents circuit geometry and named-corner coordinates as derived from OpenStreetMap and licensed under the Open Database Licence (ODbL).

Source project: https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas

Source GeoJSON files:
- https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/blob/main/tracks/spa-francorchamps/raw/layers/gp.geojson
- https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/blob/main/tracks/monza/raw/layers/gp.geojson
- https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/blob/main/tracks/laguna-seca/raw/layers/gp.geojson
- https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/blob/main/tracks/silverstone/raw/layers/gp.geojson
- https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/blob/main/tracks/monaco/raw/layers/gp.geojson
- https://github.com/tobi/track-atlas/blob/main/tracks/suzuka/raw/layers/gp.geojson

Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Data licence: Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0 — https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The WGS84 outline coordinates are projected into a local metre-based rendering frame and normalised to each circuit's published lap length. The Suzuka bridge deck and vertical separation are an in-game elevation interpretation of the published figure-eight crossover, not a survey-grade 3D scan.
